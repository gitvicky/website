Train and integrate the response surrogates

Define physically related case groups and label a small baseline. Keep entire groups together in training and holdout sets. Inspect failed labels, sampled domains and physical integrals. After the baseline, agree held-out accuracy metrics and targets with the owner.

Train structured EC ensembles and evaluate untouched holdouts. The selected pretrained ITER pedestal is ported directly; no second network needs to learn its outputs. Integrate source and boundary responses into TORAX behind guards with Tier A fallbacks. Supply channel adapters required by the selected targets. Check gradients, finite execution, energy/particle balance, physical rejection, fallback use and coupled cost. Pointwise profile errors alone do not establish absorbed-power accuracy.

Done when: the reference datasets, checkpoints, accuracy results, guarded coupled checks and matched cost measurements have provenance and meet the agreed criteria.
