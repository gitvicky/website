Checked exports

They establish storage consistency, not proved physical behavior (OQ-23).

Direct heat diagnostic route

An original native driver links the byte-bound TGLF library, reads the complete generated input and executes one local-state solve. Full-precision text retains particle and heat flux by species, low-k heat totals, the native ky grid and weights, growth/frequency pairs, and saturated and quasilinear heat spectra by species, field, ky and mode. An independent synthetic branch fills those arrays without reading an equilibrium or calling the solver. The strict reader rejects missing, extra, nonfinite or dimensionally inconsistent records.

The paired route runs the pinned in-memory wrapper in a fresh process with the same complete defaults, global settings and prepared local inputs. Input precedence matches the reference worker. Native shape parameter names omit the wrapper’s local suffix; mapping them preserves register values and the native generated-file audit checks all settings. No parser default silently replaces a declared value.

For the declared logarithmic ky grid, each interval uses D = log(k1/k0)/(k1-k0), w1 = k1*(1-k0*D) and w0 = k0*(k1*D-1), with first weights zero and the first ky. The contribution is w0*previous_spectrum + w1*current_spectrum. Sum all fields, modes and intervals for total heat; native low-k heat retains phi and perpendicular magnetic fields through ky at most one. Keep all signed contributions and compare totals at mixed relative/absolute tolerance 1e-12 in gyro-Bohm units.

Separate comparisons report native versus paired species fluxes, and paired electron/total-ion flux versus the saved inverse conversion. Any disagreement is retained as diagnostic evidence. The native sheared double-pass eigenvalues may retain unsheared first-pass values; growth/frequency pairs alone neither label a mode physically nor prove numerical convergence. Solver status and finite output checks supply only the supported numerical diagnostics.

Basis-role results

The completed grid passes its saved-output audit and reproduces all consumed controls (2139 integrity checks pass on the complete 54-pair basis grid and all 36 consumed-control comparisons reproduce the preceding study; electron flux changes sign at every state, steps reach 4.28 gB along the final basis and 4.11 gB along the search basis, and at the largest final basis the flux is a residual of order 1e-3 gBC-205). Up to half of unstable cells select the upper width bound. These are register observations; they identify no physical branch, cause or correct setting.
