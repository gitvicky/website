Scope

M4 separates formal properties over declared domains, statistical calibration and closed-loop comparisons. The owner-selected scope is OQ-22. No M4 certification review has occurred and no formal property is yet certified.

Checked exports

tkit.verify.export makes fixed float64 PyTorch copies of the Equinox networks. It includes input transforms, normalization and physical output transforms. Transport’s served response averages members in training space before inverse signed-log; EC clamps the raw mean after averaging. The selected pedestal retains its power-law residual, tanh-GELU, squared outputs and density scaling, with every solution explicit.

check_parity rejects nonfinite arrays, shape differences and a discrepancy beyond the selected numeric criterion. OQ-26 approves mixed relative and absolute parity after the relative-only check exposed cancellation near zero: 13 export checks pass in each mode: all selected members, random box points, local holdouts, physical transforms and served means, with rtol=1e-12 and atol=1e-12 in each declared output unitC-180. Formal proofs may not bypass this gate. The artifact manifests bind model, metadata and explicit box files and allow transport exports to load without opening the large training datasets. They establish storage consistency, not proved physical behavior (OQ-23).

Property contracts

six property contracts load; 14 property-validation tests pass in each mode, including malformed-domain, tolerance and output-schema rejectionC-177. tkit.verify.properties validates model scopes, output names, finite ordered boxes, the declared box data source, robustness tolerances and owner decisions. The existing OQ-20 monotonicity contract is retained.

The formal engine is auto_LiRPA with an input-splitting loop under OQ-24. The loop and proof certificates are later work. Bounds target every member; local robustness targets the served ensemble response. Launcher families stay categorical, and complete perturbation boxes that leave a training domain will be reported separately. No training box is silently shrunk to make a property pass.

Closed-loop comparison

tkit.verify.closed_loop reads provenance-bound saved runs and rejects unmatched settings, time/radial grids, nonfinite profiles or unconverged solver records. It checks that a face served by the surrogate was served by TGLF in the reference.

The historical paper eq. 41 convention is retained:

\[ E = 100\frac{\sqrt{\operatorname{mean}((T_{sur}-T_{ref})^2)}} {\operatorname{mean}(T_{sur})}. \]

The guard is defined on faces, while temperatures are cell-centred. Both temperatures are linearly interpolated to the same served faces. Whole-profile errors and a supplementary comparison using cells with both adjacent faces served remain visible. The saved runs’ grids are uniform; the reader rejects other grids without explicit face coordinates.

ITER passes (served-face T_e 7.42%, T_i 8.18%, both passing the 15% limit; whole profile 7.72/9.23%; 34 of 51 final-state faces servedC-178). MAST-U fails (served-face T_e 35.33%, T_i 49.15%, both failing 15%; whole profile 41.51/62.39%; cells with both adjacent faces served give 11.86/23.24%, still failing T_i; 13 of 26 final-state faces servedC-179), with the conservative cell diagnostic also failing ion temperature. Its failure remains ceiling K-030. The mask is only the final accepted state’s guard diagnostic; it does not measure fallback calls throughout the trajectory.

MAST-U remediation diagnosis

The owner prioritizes the failed MAST-U criterion (OQ-25). Matched final-state probes show substantial flux error: all 17 in-domain final-state probes succeed; on 12 served faces with reference flux above 1 gB, median relative errors are 41.17% Q_e and 74.27% Q_i; an unconstrained v3 control gives 38.05/71.88%C-181. The existing unconstrained v3 model also misses those feedback states. That control is diagnostic and does not meet the owner’s monotonicity contract; pointwise scores are not a coupled comparison.

The accepted-state replay reproduces the failed saved trajectory: all saved temperature, density and psi values are reproduced exactly; four final inner faces exceed the existing ion-diffusivity guard bound; 44.81% of accepted-state face diagnostics reject the surrogateC-182. Four final inner faces lie in the model’s box but exceed the ion-diffusivity guard bound. Captured masks cover accepted states, not the solver’s intermediate evaluations. The isolated inner served face has zero final heat flux in both model and TGLF even though its temperature differs. No face is removed from the acceptance comparison.

The approved refresh collected feedback-state labels on training equilibria only, with both test equilibria excluded. It retains full solver inputs and applies the approved-region filter. The local result review checks the finished artifacts: eight local checks pass for approved allocation/time, hashes and lineage, full-input alignment, case budget, unique coordinates, training-only equilibria, source model and finite successful labelsC-183. It compares each label input row with the selected case and the source trajectory’s accepted-state solver arrays, and binds lineage by hash. These checks establish collection integrity; they do not establish held-out accuracy or a successful coupled remedy. The owner approved artifact synchronization in OQ-28. We checked remote privacy settings and downloaded every uploaded record by artifact identifier: all 26 uploaded artifact records covering 18 distinct files match both remote SHA-256 metadata and downloaded bytes; the folder and private visibility match the approved request, and the offline payload queue is emptyC-184. Duplicate provenance sidecars are retained as distinct records; name-based retrieval is ambiguous for those duplicates. Downloads by identifier verify every record without changing the remote artifacts. Retraining and an improved closed-loop result remain pending. No new certificate or domain is deployed, and the 15% tolerance is unchanged.
