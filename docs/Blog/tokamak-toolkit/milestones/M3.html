M3 · Surrogates

Done 10 Oct 2026

Goal

Train neural surrogates for the expensive physics, starting with turbulent transport: deep ensembles in JAX with physics structure built in (zero flux below threshold, positivity, monotonicity in the gradients), running inside the M1 coupling loop behind the guard. Then RF deposition and the pedestal. The transport surrogates learn TGLF from the M2 datasets, one ensemble per machine, MAST-U and ITER.

Acceptance criteria (fixed in the brief before work started)

  1. Held-out accuracy meets the target in the configuration.
  2. The surrogate is differentiable and runs inside the M1 loop.
  3. The end-to-end speed-up is measured and reported.

M3 has no human gate in the brief. The brief left the accuracy target to the configuration; the owner chose to set it after a first baseline (OQ-18). It was set on 3 Oct 2026 (OQ-19): median relative error of the ensemble mean at most 0.30 on Qe and on Qi (cases with |Q| above 1 gyro-Bohm unit), on each machine’s held-out split of dataset v3. The final monotone transport ensembles now pass that target: MAST-U: Q_e 17.8%, Q_i 25.2%; ITER: Q_e 7.2%, Q_i 10.8%; all meet the 30% targetC-129. The milestone remains in progress.

Closure

All three criteria have evidence for every required surrogate (audit); the MAST-U end-to-end timing was measured last (TGLF 364.1 s, surrogate 0.492 s, Bohm/gyro-Bohm 0.043 s: the surrogate is about 740 times faster than TGLF; profile deviation from TGLF at 0.2 s, T_e 41.5% and T_i 62.4% (Bohm/gyro-Bohm 47.9% and 84.6%), with half the faces outside the training window and on the fallbackC-176). The owner confirmed the closure on 10 Oct 2026 (entry 41). Open ceilings carry into M4.

Deviations so far

  • ITER pedestal uses the owner-selected pretrained single network. Its JAX port has numerical parity and derivative checks (12 upstream scalar regression values agree within relative tolerance 1e-12C-149, 12 focused tests pass in full and Tier-A-only modesC-150), with no ensemble uncertainty inferred. MAST-U predictive closure and IC are deferred.
  • 20 members for the TGLF-NN variants (as in GA’s model files), above the brief’s 5–10. The baseline has 10.
  • Active learning moved from M2 to M3 (OQ-18 a), since it needs a trained ensemble to choose the points.
  • Monotonicity imposed where TGLF itself is not monotone (OQ-20 1): the surrogate departs from TGLF there by construction: MAST-U: Q_e 3.14, Q_i 3.36; ITER: Q_e 2.51, Q_i 2.11 signed-log unitsC-131. Sampled monotonicity checks are not a formal certificate.
  • Accuracy target measured on dataset v3, not on the M2 datasets (OQ-19); errors on the M2 test splits are reported, not gated.
